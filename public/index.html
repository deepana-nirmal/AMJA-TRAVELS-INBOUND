Who we are

A Colombo travel house, arranging Sri Lanka since 1998

Amja Travels Inbound is the local, ground-handling side of Amja Travels. We build the whole trip for visitors to Sri Lanka — the route, the driver-guide, the hotels and every transfer — and run it from our own team on the island, not a call centre abroad.

Accredited by IATA, TAASL and the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka. One point of contact from your first enquiry to your airport drop-off.

Ways to travel

Build your trip around an experience

Heritage

Culture & History

Rock fortresses, cave temples, royal cities and the sacred tooth relic in Kandy — over two thousand years of it.

Scenic

Hills & Tea Country

The blue-train ride to Ella, plantation walks, cool mornings and waterfalls in every valley.

Wild

Wildlife & Safari

Leopards in Yala, elephant gatherings at Minneriya, blue whales off Mirissa and birdlife everywhere.

Coast

Beaches & Ocean

Calm bays on the south and west, surf on the east, snorkelling reefs and long quiet stretches of sand.

Active

Adventure & Outdoors

White-water rafting at Kitulgala, hikes to World's End, cycling through paddy fields and cave exploring.

Tailor-made

Private & Family

Your own vehicle and guide throughout — flexible days, family-friendly pacing and stops on request.
